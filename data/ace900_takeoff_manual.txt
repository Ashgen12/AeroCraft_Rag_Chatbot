**AeroCraft ACE-900 Operations Manual: Normal Procedures - Takeoff**

**Section ID: NP-TO**

**Overview:**
This section outlines the standard procedures for takeoff in the AeroCraft ACE-900 under normal operating conditions. Adherence to these steps ensures safety and optimal aircraft performance.

**Phase 1: Pre-Takeoff Checklist (ID: NP-TO-P1)**
*   **Action:** Perform Pre-Takeoff Checklist items thoroughly.
*   **Key Items:**
    *   Brakes (ID: NP-TO-P1-ITEM1): Check SET. Status: Checked.
    *   Flaps (ID: NP-TO-P1-ITEM2): Set to 15 DEGREES. Target Value: 15 deg.
    *   Engine Instruments (ID: NP-TO-P1-ITEM3): Check ALL GREEN. Status: Normal.
    *   Flight Controls (ID: NP-TO-P1-ITEM4): Check FREE AND CORRECT movement. Status: Checked.
    *   Transponder (ID: NP-TO-P1-ITEM5): Set ALTITUDE REPORTING mode. Mode: ALT.

**Phase 2: Taxiing (ID: NP-TO-P2)**
*   **Action:** Taxi aircraft to the assigned runway.
*   **Parameters:**
    *   Taxi Speed (ID: NP-TO-P2-PARAM1): Maintain maximum 20 KNOTS. Limit: 20 kts.
    *   Steering (ID: NP-TO-P2-PARAM2): Use RUDDER PEDALS primarily, supplement with DIFFERENTIAL BRAKING if needed. Control: Rudder/Brakes.
    *   Lookout (ID: NP-TO-P2-PARAM3): Maintain constant VIGILANCE for other traffic and obstacles. Action: Scan.

**Phase 3: Before Takeoff (ID: NP-TO-P3)**
*   **Action:** Position aircraft on runway and prepare for takeoff roll.
*   **Steps:**
    *   Line Up (ID: NP-TO-P3-STEP1): Align aircraft with RUNWAY CENTERLINE. Action: Align.
    *   Brakes (ID: NP-TO-P3-STEP2): HOLD firmly. Action: Hold.
    *   Engine Power (ID: NP-TO-P3-STEP3): Set TAKEOFF POWER (Approximately 95% N1). Target Value: 95% N1.
    *   Annunciators (ID: NP-TO-P3-STEP4): Check ALL CLEAR (No warning/caution lights). Status: Clear.
    *   Release Brakes (ID: NP-TO-P3-STEP5): Release SMOOTHLY upon confirmation of stable power and clear annunciators. Action: Release.

**Phase 4: Takeoff Roll (ID: NP-TO-P4)**
*   **Action:** Accelerate aircraft down the runway.
*   **Procedures:**
    *   Directional Control (ID: NP-TO-P4-PROC1): Maintain CENTERLINE using RUDDER. Control: Rudder.
    *   Airspeed Monitoring (ID: NP-TO-P4-PROC2): Monitor airspeed indicator closely. Action: Monitor.
    *   Rotate Speed (Vr) (ID: NP-TO-P4-PROC3): Defined as 75 KNOTS. Target Value: 75 kts.
    *   Rotation (ID: NP-TO-P4-PROC4): At Vr, apply smooth back pressure on CONTROL COLUMN to lift nosewheel. Action: Rotate.

**Phase 5: Initial Climb (ID: NP-TO-P5)**
*   **Action:** Establish positive climb profile after liftoff.
*   **Steps:**
    *   Landing Gear (ID: NP-TO-P5-STEP1): RETRACT once positive rate of climb is established. Action: Retract Gear. Condition: Positive Climb Rate.
    *   Flaps (ID: NP-TO-P5-STEP2): RETRACT passing 400 FT AGL and minimum safe maneuvering speed. Action: Retract Flaps. Condition: >400ft AGL & Safe Speed.
    *   Climb Speed (Vy) (ID: NP-TO-P5-STEP3): Establish climb at 85 KNOTS for best rate of climb. Target Value: 85 kts.
    *   Power Adjustment (ID: NP-TO-P5-STEP4): Reduce to CLIMB POWER (Approximately 90% N1) as appropriate. Target Value: 90% N1.

**End of Section NP-TO**
